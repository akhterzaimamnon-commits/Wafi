UAE Corporate Tax readiness check

Know where you stand, before the FTA asks.

Question 1 of 4

Is your business a company or an individual?

This means a juridical person (company) versus a natural person (sole individual carrying on business).

Question 2 of 4

Are you in a Free Zone?

This means your business is registered and licensed in a UAE Free Zone, not the mainland.

Question 3 of 4

What is your annual revenue?

Use your total annual business revenue, not profit.

Question 4 of 4

What is your financial year-end?

Most UAE companies use 31 December. Your deadline is based on this date.
