# Wafi
Free UAE Corporate Tax readiness tool. Helps businesses determine CT registration, regime, audit requirements, and deadlines.

## What it does

Answers four questions and tells you:

- Whether you must register for Corporate Tax
- Which regime applies (Standard, Small Business Relief, or Qualifying Free Zone Person)
- Whether audited financial statements are required
- Your Corporate Tax filing deadline

## Who it's for

UAE businesses, accountants, and anyone who needs a plain-language answer to "what does Corporate Tax mean for me?"

## How it works

- No login
- No data stored
- Everything runs in your browser
- Rules sourced from Federal Tax Authority and Ministry of Finance decisions

This is not tax advice. It is a plain-language guide based on general rules. Rules change, and your exact situation may differ. Confirm with the Federal Tax Authority or a licensed tax agent before filing.
